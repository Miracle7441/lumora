Our Studio

We believe interiors should feel like a deep breath.

Founded in 2016 by architect Sofia Marin, Lumora began as a two-person atelier in Lisbon.

We don't chase trends. We build calm, tactile spaces that age gracefully - where every joint and shadow line is considered.

Mission: To create interiors that restore clarity.

Vision: Luxury measured by time, craft, and care.

Values: Material honesty, slow design, craft over decoration.
